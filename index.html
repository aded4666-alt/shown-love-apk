Message from Samuel

No helping hand, she stands alone
Yet makes a house a loving home.
Her heart is soft, your will is stone
Her love has beautifully grown.

She hides her tears, she hides her pain
She dances proudly in the rain.
For her kids, she'll bear the strain
And finds her joy in their small gain.

She is a mother, father too, so strong, so kind, so brave, so true. There's nothing that she cannot do, Her love will always see you through.

So here's to her, the queen, the guide,

Message from Joseph

Since I was small, it's been you and us two,
No father beside us, just your love so true.
Your hands worked hard, your heart worked more,
You gave us the world, though you had little in store.

You hid your pain.

You were mother and father in one soul,
Playing both parts, making us whole.
No complaint, no excuse, just grace,
Even when tired, you kept the pace.

You taught us that love is more than what you feel.
Single by title, but double in strength,
You loved us beyond all length.

Now your birthday comes, let the heavens see,
The queen who made a man out of me.
If I had a thousand lifetimes to live,
All my love, Mum, to you I would give.

You are not just my mother, you are my home,
The reason I will never walk alone.
Happy birthday, my hero, my heart so true,
Everything I am, Mum, I owe to you.

— Joseph 💛
